Lead Quality Audit — Exterior Services, Saskatchewan

Client reporting counted raw form submissions as leads, bundling spam, duplicates and job applications with genuine estimate requests and making cost per lead unknowable. Audited every submission individually, classified each entry, verified tracking end to end, and rebuilt reporting around verified leads only.

Result: 618 verified estimate leads established across 13 months, with 94 job applications separated into their own pipeline. Record single month of 103 leads, up 74% year on year.

Technical Audit Adopted by Client — Real Estate, British Columbia

A technically sophisticated client had rebuilt his own 273-page site using AI coding agents and was questioning the value of external SEO support. Delivered four findings with business consequences attached: a stale cached homepage being served by Google, three analytics tools with no verified lead counting, a contact form on custom code at risk of silent failure, and 46 near-duplicate city pages diluting local relevance.

Result: Client actioned every finding, resubmitted the sitemap, requested reindexing on priority pages, and confirmed the audit's value in writing. Budget was redirected toward local authority building and AI search visibility.

Dynamic Schema Deployment at Portfolio Scale

Structured data was missing or invalid across a large client portfolio, and neither hand-written schema nor plugin-generated markup was viable at scale. Built dynamic PHP schema injected at template level, pulling business data programmatically so markup stays accurate site-wide.

Result: Valid, consistent structured data across the portfolio with centralised maintenance, improving rich result eligibility and strengthening the entity signals that now feed AI citation.
