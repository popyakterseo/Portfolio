Selected Work
CS / 01 16 Months of Compounding Growth — Saskatoon Dental Practice +
Challenge

Willowgreen Dental was ranking for 616 keywords but only 17 of them sat in the top three, with organic traffic flat at 818 monthly visits. Visibility existed; conversion-adjacent visibility did not. The practice competed against every general dentist in Saskatoon for the same generic terms.

Approach

Technical foundations first — crawl and indexation fixes, structured data across every service page. Then content architecture: service pages mapped to procedure-level intent rather than broad terms, supported by a monthly editorial cycle with client approval built in. Local SEO ran in parallel, with map-pack tracking on a 2.5km radius. Off-page work built referring domains steadily rather than in spikes. A tightly scoped Google Ads campaign covered high-intent terms while organic compounded.

Outcome

Traffic grew more than fivefold and top-three rankings went from 17 to 134 across sixteen months. Eight core service terms reached position one, including root canal, veneers and family dental clinic. The Ads campaign returned an 11.43% click-through rate at $39.34 per conversion — well inside benchmark for the sector.

CS / 02 Auditing Lead Volume Down to Lead Truth — Everflow Exteriors +
Challenge

Everflow's reporting counted raw form submissions as leads. That number looked healthy but told the client nothing useful — it bundled spam, duplicate entries and job applications together with real estimate requests, so cost-per-lead was unknowable and budget decisions were being made on a figure nobody had verified.

Approach

I audited every submission individually rather than trusting the form plugin's count. Each entry was classified as a genuine estimate request, a duplicate, spam, or a job application, then reconciled against the analytics record. The tracking itself was tested end to end to confirm real enquiries were reaching the client and being recorded. From there, reporting shifted to verified leads only, with applications tracked separately as a recruitment signal.

Outcome

An honest baseline: 618 verified estimate leads across thirteen months, and 94 job applications separated out into their own pipeline. June 2026 set a record at 103 leads in a single month, up 74% year over year. When I later proposed a service-area expansion, the client declined on capacity grounds — the right answer, and one the accurate numbers made possible.

CS / 03 A Technical Audit the Client Chose to Act On — Corbin & Co +
Challenge

A Langley real estate client had rebuilt his own 273-page site using AI coding agents and automated most of his content and reporting. He was technically capable, confident in his stack, and openly questioning what an SEO team could still add. The honest answer had to be specific or it would not land.

Approach

I audited the live site and returned four findings with business consequences attached rather than a list of errors. Google was serving a cached homepage with an outdated headline and form. Three analytics tools were running simultaneously with no verified confirmation that a single real lead was being counted. The contact form depended on custom code that would fail silently on the next plugin update, with no alert. And 46 of 273 pages targeted cities with near-identical copy and no genuine connection to the Langley brand — a duplication pattern that quietly erodes local relevance. I framed the underlying argument plainly: automation still needs a human watching it, because a form that stops working or a page ranking for the wrong city does more damage than no automation at all.

Outcome

The client accepted every finding and ran the cleanup himself — metadata and title duplication, utility-page indexing, nofollow and empty-anchor issues, thin content, a dead internal link — then resubmitted the sitemap and requested reindexing on priority pages. He confirmed the value of the audit in writing. The forward proposal shifted budget from now-automated content toward local authority building, AI-search visibility, and SEO foundations for two divisions launching later in the year.

CS / 04 Building for Citation, Not Just Ranking — GEO & AI Search +
Challenge

A growing share of commercial queries now resolve inside an AI answer rather than a click. For local service businesses, that means the question "who is the best dentist in Saskatoon" can be answered without the ranking page ever being visited. Classical position tracking does not measure this, so the risk is invisible until the traffic is already gone.

Approach

I restructure content so that language models can extract and attribute it: direct answers placed in the opening lines, entity-rich passages that establish who the business is and where it operates, comparison tables, definitional blocks and FAQ sections shaped the way answer engines quote them. Underneath that sits the technical layer — clean schema, consistent entity signals, and crawler accessibility for AI agents alongside traditional bots. Off-page work targets the third-party sources these systems tend to cite, since trust is inherited as much as earned. Visibility is then monitored by querying ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Overviews directly for the commercial prompts a real buyer would use, and checking who gets named.

Outcome

A dual-channel approach that holds classical rankings while building presence on generative surfaces — and, just as importantly, a way to see what is happening there. Willowgreen currently surfaces on 56 AI-attributed queries and 26 SERP features. This is a fast-moving discipline and I treat it as one: monitored, tested, and revised against what the engines actually do rather than what the industry assumes.

CS / 05 Dynamic PHP Schema Across a 50-Site Portfolio +
Challenge

Structured data was missing or inconsistent across a large client portfolio, limiting rich-result eligibility. Hand-writing schema per page was not viable at that scale, and plugin-generated markup was generic and often invalid.

Approach

Built dynamic PHP-based schema injected at the template level inside the WordPress backend, pulling business data programmatically so LocalBusiness, Service and Organization markup stayed accurate site-wide without per-page maintenance. Validated output and monitored eligibility in Search Console.

Outcome

Consistent, valid structured data across the portfolio, improved rich-result eligibility, and clearer entity signals — the same signals that now feed AI citation. One implementation, deployed everywhere, maintained centrally.

CS / 06 Full Site Rebuilds, Demo to Launch +
Challenge

Several clients across HVAC, home building and heating had sites that ranked poorly and converted worse — dated layouts, weak local signals, and contact paths that lost visitors. Each rebuild had to satisfy an owner with strong opinions and limited patience for process.

Approach

I ran these end to end: staged demo build, structured feedback round with the owner, then launch. Service pages were written to keyword priorities the client set themselves, built in HTML with schema and internal linking in place from the start rather than retrofitted. Feedback was actioned specifically — button behaviour, copy changes, form field order, service-area lists reworked to remove redundant sub-areas while preserving the ones carrying local search value.

Outcome

Live, indexed, conversion-oriented sites with owner sign-off on record. On one home builder account, five priority service pages went live in a single cycle against the client's own stated keyword ranking — major renovations, custom shops, garages, barns and main floor renovations — and the client immediately asked for the same treatment in a second city.

CS / 07 Custom Tooling & Workflow Automation +
Challenge

Repetitive technical work — image conversion, geo-tagging, on-page checks, lead notification — consumed hours that should have gone to strategy, and manual handling introduced errors at portfolio scale.

Approach

Built internal tools to remove the repetition: an unlimited WebP converter, a geo-tagging utility, and an SEO analyser. Separately developed a universal WordPress lead-notification plugin supporting Elementor Pro, Contact Form 7, WPForms, Gravity Forms and Fluent Forms, with a three-step setup wizard, automatic chat ID detection, and no hardcoded credentials — designed for reuse across any client site.

Outcome

Faster optimisation cycles, fewer manual errors, and reusable infrastructure rather than one-off fixes. Capacity moved from repetition to strategy.
