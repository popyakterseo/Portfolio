Case studies

Selected Work
CS / 01 Multi-Site SEO Leadership across 50+ Websites +
Challenge

Managing SEO strategy across multiple websites with varying performance levels and inconsistent technical foundations.

Approach

Full technical audits, crawlability and indexing fixes, structured data implementation, backend internal linking overhaul, metadata and content restructuring, and monthly KPI-based reporting.

Outcome

Improved site health scores, consistent organic visibility gains, stronger crawl efficiency, and enhanced reporting transparency for stakeholders.

CS / 02 Dynamic PHP Schema Implementation at Scale +
Challenge

Client websites lacked structured data, limiting search visibility and rich result eligibility.

Approach

Built dynamic PHP-based schema deployed site-wide with structured LocalBusiness and Service schema, integrated directly within the WordPress backend.

Outcome

Enhanced rich result eligibility, improved SERP click-through rates, and clearer indexation signals across the portfolio.

CS / 03 Automation & Custom SEO Tool Development +
Challenge

Manual SEO workflows were inefficient and time-consuming, slowing delivery and increasing error rates.

Approach

Developed three internal efficiency tools: an Unlimited WebP Converter, a GeoTag Tool, and an SEO Analyzer Tool to automate repeat tasks.

Outcome

Reduced repetitive workload, accelerated technical optimization cycles, and freed team capacity for strategic work.

CS / 04 Paid Media Audit & SEO Channel Alignment +
Challenge

Paid advertising performance lacked alignment with the organic strategy, creating funnel friction.

Approach

Audited Ads reports against CTR, CPC, and ROAS benchmarks. Proposed landing page and keyword alignment to bridge SEO and paid into a single integration strategy.

Outcome

Cleaner funnel logic, stronger cross-channel growth strategy, and better traffic quality alignment across both channels.

CS / 05 AEO & GEO Content Strategy Built to Beat the SERP +
Challenge

Classical SEO content was losing visibility to AI overviews and answer engines, with traffic shifting to zero-click surfaces.

Approach

Restructured content around AEO & GEO principles — entity-rich passages, direct answer formatting, semantic clustering, and AI-assisted research at velocity. Combined classical ranking factors with answer-engine readability.

Outcome

Content surfacing across answer engines and AI overviews while retaining classical ranking strength — a dual-channel organic strategy that compounds against Google updates.

CS / 06 6-Month Off-Page Strategy — Canadian Multi-Location Brand +
Challenge

A Canadian printing brand with 15 city locations needed authority signals competitive with national players, plus local trust at the map-pack level.

Approach

Built a phased six-month off-page roadmap: foundation citations (NAP, GBP, social profiles), Canadian .ca directory placement, niche printing & design citations, editorial guest post outreach to DR 35+ Canadian business media, plus geo-targeted link acquisition. Tracked against monthly KPI targets for referring domains, DR 40+ links, and Canadian-domain ratio.

Outcome

A documented, KPI-tracked authority-building system — moving from baseline to projected 200–260 referring domains and 35–50 DR 40+ links across six months, with built-in local relevance for every target city.
